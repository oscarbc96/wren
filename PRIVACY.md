# Privacy

Wren is a desktop browser. The project runs no servers for it and receives none
of your data.

- **All data stays on your device.** History, bookmarks, session state and
  settings live in a local store. Secrets live only in the macOS Keychain.
- **No telemetry.** Wren collects no analytics, usage data or crash reports and
  sends none anywhere.
- **Network calls.** Wren loads the pages you ask for. Besides that, the only
  network calls are the ones you enable, such as update checks and AI features.
- **AI features.** When you use an AI feature, data is sent only to the
  provider you configure, and only the data that feature needs. You choose the
  provider and can use a local one.
- **Deletion.** Removing a profile deletes its database, keychain entries and
  caches. Nothing is held by the project.
